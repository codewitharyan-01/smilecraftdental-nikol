Questions

The things people actually ask.

Written the way we would answer on the phone — including the ones about money, which most clinic websites avoid.

General

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Appointments are better — you will not sit waiting while someone else’s root canal overruns. That said, if you are in pain, call or message and we will fit you in the same day wherever possible.

What are your timings?

Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Closed on Sundays. Evening slots after 6 PM go first, so message a day or two ahead if that is the only time you can come.

Where exactly are you?

Shop 214, Second Floor, Blueberry Complex, above National Handloom House, near Virat Nagar Road, beside Bhojaldham Society, Nikol, Ahmedabad 380049. Take the lift to the second floor.

How do I pay?

Cash, UPI and cards. For larger plans — implants, orthodontics, full-mouth work — payment can be split across the treatment. Ask for the written estimate first.

Cost

How much does root canal treatment cost in Ahmedabad?

It depends on the tooth. A front tooth usually has one canal and costs less; a molar has three or four and takes longer. Retreatment of a previously root-filled tooth costs more again, and a crown is additional.

We do not publish a single number, because it would be wrong for most people. Come in, get the tooth examined and radiographed, and you will have an itemised figure before anything begins.

How much do dental implants cost in Gujarat?

Implants are priced as several components: the fixture itself, any bone grafting, the abutment, and the crown on top. Different implant systems and crown materials account for most of the variation between clinics, and quoted prices are rarely comparing the same thing.

Ask any clinic for a written breakdown listing each component and the brand of the implant. That is the only way to compare fairly.

How much does it cost to remove a wisdom tooth?

An erupted wisdom tooth is a straightforward extraction. An impacted one — still under the gum, or lying sideways against the molar in front — is a surgical procedure and costs more. A radiograph decides which you are looking at.

Is there a consultation fee?

Consultations are charged, and the fee is adjusted against treatment if you go ahead. Ask when you book — the reception will tell you the exact figure for your case.

Treatment

Will it hurt?

Treatment is done under local anaesthesia, so it should not be painful. The injection itself is the part most people dread; we use a topical numbing gel first and give it slowly. Afterwards, tenderness for a day or two is normal and a painkiller covers it.

How many sittings will I need?

A filling or a scaling is one visit. A root canal is one or two, plus a crown. An implant is a consultation, a placement, then a crown months later. Orthodontics runs for a year or two with reviews every month or two. You will be told at the planning stage.

Can I get treatment during pregnancy?

Routine cleaning and urgent treatment are generally fine, particularly in the second trimester. Elective cosmetic work and non-urgent X-rays are better delayed. Tell us, and we will plan around it — and coordinate with your obstetrician where needed.

Do you treat children?

Yes. First visits, fluoride, sealants, fillings and habit advice. The first appointment is a look and a chat, ideally before anything hurts.

What if I need a specialist you don’t have?

Most treatment is done in-house. For a small number of cases — complex jaw surgery, certain orthodontic discrepancies — we refer to people we know and trust, and we will tell you who and why.
