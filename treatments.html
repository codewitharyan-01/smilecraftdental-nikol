Restorative & surgical

Restorative & surgical

Fixing what is broken, and replacing what cannot be saved.

01Dental implantsA titanium post in the jaw, topped with a crown. Single tooth, several, or a full arch.02Root canal treatmentCleaning out an infected nerve, sealing the canals, then protecting the tooth with a crown.03Crowns, bridges & capsZirconia or metal-ceramic coverage for cracked, heavily filled or root-treated teeth.04Wisdom tooth removalImpacted third molars, including surgical removal under local anaesthesia.05Simple & surgical extractionsWhen a tooth genuinely cannot be saved, and what to do in the two weeks after.06Full-mouth rehabilitationMultiple problems rebuilt in a planned sequence, usually over several months.

Cosmetic dentistry

For teeth that are healthy but you would rather they looked different.

07Smile design & veneersShape, shade and proportion planned first — then built in ceramic or composite.08Teeth whiteningIn-clinic bleaching with shade documentation, or a slower take-home kit.09Composite bondingResin built up directly on the tooth for chips, small gaps and worn edges.10Gum contouringReshaping the gum line where it sits unevenly or too low over the front teeth.

Orthodontics

Orthodontics

Straightening teeth, at fifteen or at fifty. Both are common here.

11Metal bracesThe most predictable route for complex crowding and bite correction.12Ceramic bracesSame mechanics as metal, with tooth-coloured brackets and lighter wires.13Clear alignersRemovable trays changed every week or two. Needs discipline: 20–22 hours a day.14RetainersFixed or removable, after treatment. Not optional, however straight things look.

Preventive & children

Preventive & children

The cheapest dentistry there is: catching things early.

15Check-up & X-raysA proper look, a radiograph where indicated, and a written plan if anything is found.16Scaling & polishingCalculus and stain removal, gum assessment and honest instruction on what to do at home.17FillingsTooth-coloured composite for decay caught early, before it reaches the nerve.18Children’s dentistryFirst visits, fluoride, sealants and habit advice — ideally before anything hurts.19Night guardsFor grinding and jaw clenching that is flattening the back teeth.
