Finding the door

Blueberry Complex is the building with National Handloom House on the ground floor, on the Virat Nagar Road side. Take the lift or stairs to the second floor; shop 214 is along the corridor. There is two-wheeler parking on the road and car parking inside the complex.

Call if it is urgent

For severe pain, swelling or a knocked-out tooth, call rather than message. If we are with a patient, leave a voice note and we will call back the same day.

Outside hours

Sundays are closed. For a dental emergency on a Sunday, message on WhatsApp anyway — we will tell you honestly whether it can wait until Monday.
