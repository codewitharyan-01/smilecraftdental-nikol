Questions

Before you book.

All questions
Do I need an appointment?

Yes, ideally. Message on WhatsApp or call and we will find a slot — often the same day if you are in pain. Sunday is closed.

Will treatment hurt?

We use a numbing gel before the injection and give it slowly, so the injection is the least of it. Most people take a painkiller for a day afterwards and are fine.

What does a root canal cost?

It depends on the tooth: a front tooth usually has one canal, a molar three or four. We examine, take a radiograph, and give you an itemised figure before starting.

Do you take cards and UPI?

Yes — cash, UPI and cards. Larger plans such as implants or braces can be split across sittings.

Before you book

Three things worth knowing.

What a visit costs

We don’t publish a price list, because the same tooth can need two different plans. You get an itemised figure after the examination, before anything begins.

  • Consultation fee, adjusted against treatment
  • Written estimate you can take away
  • Cash, UPI and cards; larger plans split

Your first visit

Thirty to forty-five minutes, mostly talking and looking. Nothing is drilled unless you are in pain and ask us to treat it that day.

  • Bring old X-rays or a previous plan
  • Bring your medicine list
  • Bring a parent for anyone under 18

If you are nervous

Tell us at the start — on WhatsApp if saying it out loud is harder. It changes how the appointment runs: shorter, with breaks, and the chair kept upright.

  • Numbing gel before any injection
  • Raise your left hand to stop, any time
  • Come and look around before booking

In pain right now?

Call rather than message — we keep a slot free each day for urgent cases. Sundays are closed, so if it can wait until Monday we will tell you honestly.
