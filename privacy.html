Privacy

What we do with your information.

Short version: this website has no database. Your enquiry goes from your browser into WhatsApp. Nothing is stored on a server we control.

What the website collects

Nothing, until you type it into the enquiry form. When you submit the form, the page builds a WhatsApp message containing the name, phone number, email address, treatment, preferred date and any notes you entered, and opens WhatsApp with that message ready to send.

That message is only transmitted when you press send inside WhatsApp. Until then it sits in your own browser.

What stays in your browser

Two things, stored locally on your device using localStorage:

  • Your name, phone and email, so the form is pre-filled if you come back.
  • The page and referring link you first arrived from, so the clinic knows which channel brought you in. Only the channel name is added to the WhatsApp message.

Both are stored on your device only. Clearing your browser data removes them. The site sets no advertising cookies and does not run third-party trackers by default.

What happens after you send the message

The conversation lives in the clinic’s WhatsApp account, on the clinic’s phone. It is used to reply to you, to arrange an appointment, and for clinical records if you become a patient. It is not sold, shared or used for marketing broadcasts.

WhatsApp’s own privacy terms apply to the message itself. If you would rather not use WhatsApp, call +91 94274 51885 instead.

Clinical records

If you become a patient, your clinical records are kept by the clinic as required by the Dental Council of India and applicable Indian law. Those records are not on this website.

Your requests

You can ask us to correct or delete the details we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, at any time. Call the clinic or message on WhatsApp and say so; there is no form to fill in.

Children

Enquiries for a child should be made by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect information from children through this site.

Changes

If this note changes, the date at the bottom of the page changes with it. This version is current as of November 2026.

This page is written to be readable, not to be a legal document. If you need the clinic’s formal data-handling policy, ask at reception.
