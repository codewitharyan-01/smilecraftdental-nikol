Privacy

What happens to the details you type here.

Plain language, because a privacy notice you cannot read is not really consent.

What this website collects

Only what you type into a form: your name, mobile number, and — if you choose to give it — your email address, the treatment you are interested in, a preferred date and time, and any notes you add.

Nothing is collected automatically. There are no advertising cookies, no tracking pixels and no third-party analytics installed on this site. The map on the contact page is an embedded Google Map, which is subject to Google's own policies once it loads.

Where it goes

When you press send, your browser opens WhatsApp with the message written out. You press send there. The message arrives on the clinic's WhatsApp number and is read by the clinic's staff — the same as if you had messaged them directly.

This site has no database and no server of its own. The enquiry is not stored on the website. If the clinic has enabled the optional email delivery, a copy is also sent to the clinic's email address.

What stays on your device

Your browser keeps a draft of your name and number so that the next form is pre-filled, and remembers which page you first arrived from so the clinic knows whether you found them on Google, Instagram or a referral. Both live in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data removes them. They are never transmitted until you press send.

How long it is kept

Enquiries on WhatsApp follow WhatsApp's retention. Clinical records, once you become a patient, are kept as required for dental records in India and are separate from this website.

Your choices

  • You can ask what information the clinic holds about you, and ask for a copy.
  • You can ask for a correction, or for the enquiry to be deleted if you did not become a patient.
  • You can withdraw consent to being contacted about an enquiry at any time — reply “stop” on WhatsApp.
  • You never have to give an email address. It is optional everywhere on this site.

Who to contact

The clinic, on +91 94274 51885 or on WhatsApp, at Shop No. 214, Second Floor, Blueberry Complex (National Handloom House), Near Virat Nagar Road, beside Bhojaldham Society, Nikol, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380049.
