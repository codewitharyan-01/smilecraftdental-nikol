About the clinic

A small clinic that would rather do less, properly.

Smilecraft is a multispeciality dental practice on the second floor of Blueberry Complex in Nikol. One dentist, a handful of chairs, and enough time in the day to explain what is actually going on in your mouth.

Why we work this way

The problem with most dental visits isn’t the drill.

It’s not knowing. Not knowing whether the pain is urgent. Not knowing what the treatment will cost until you are already reclined with your mouth open. Not knowing whether the third option you were offered was necessary, or just the most expensive one.

So we do the boring, unfashionable part first: look properly, take an X-ray when it’s needed, and put the findings in front of you. Then we talk about what has to happen now, what can wait six months, and what is genuinely optional.

You will hear a number before we pick anything up. If the number changes, we stop and tell you why. That is the whole system — there is nothing clever behind it.

Sterilisation

What happens to the instruments between patients.

You are allowed to ask to see this. Most people never do, and most clinics are quietly grateful for that. Please ask.

  • Single-use, always. Needles, gloves, suction tips, bibs, cotton rolls and the plastic sleeves on the handles. Opened in front of you, disposed of after.
  • Cleaned before sterilised. Instruments are scrubbed and ultrasonically cleaned first — an autoclave cannot sterilise debris.
  • Pouched and dated. Each load is sealed in an indicator pouch, run through a Class B autoclave and stamped with the cycle date.
  • Surfaces between patients. Chair, light handle, tray and spittoon are wiped down with a hospital-grade disinfectant, not just a spray and a wipe.
  • Water lines. Lines are flushed between patients and the suction system is cleaned on a schedule, not when someone remembers.

The dentist

Dr Priya Patel

Dr Priya works across the full scope of general dentistry, with a particular focus on implants, root canal treatment and cosmetic work on the front teeth. Her consultations tend to run long, because she explains things twice — once with the X-ray, and once in plain Gujarati, Hindi or English, whichever you would rather use.

The clinic is women-owned, and it is a place where that matters less than it should have to. Everyone is welcome here, and nobody is going to be talked into treatment they did not ask about.

Paying for it

Cash, UPI and cards are all accepted. For larger plans — implants, full-mouth work, orthodontics — we can split the cost across sittings. Ask before treatment starts, not after.
